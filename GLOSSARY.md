# Glossary

Words used in this project and what they mean here. Words only, no build details.

- **Demo**: a clickable show for the Aice team. Nothing real happens. Not official Aice software.
- **Sample data ("Contoh")**: every product, price, store, address and voucher is made up and labelled "Contoh".
- **Brand**: who makes a product. Only Aice is switched on.
- **Store (outlet)**: where an order is packed. A store sells the products of the brands it lists. Brand and Store are separate on purpose.
- **Product**: one item for sale, with a size and a price.
- **Category**: a group of products: cone, tub, stick, yoghurt, drink, bundle.
- **Bundle (paket)**: a fixed pack of products sold at one price.
- **Mix pack**: a pack the shopper builds from 6, 8 or 12 cones or sticks for one set price.
- **Multi-buy ("Beli 3")**: a discount on cones when the cart holds three or more.
- **Cart**: the items picked before checkout.
- **Order**: a cart that was placed.
- **Voucher**: a code that lowers the price.
- **Delivery slot**: when the shopper wants the order to arrive.
- **Free-delivery threshold**: the basket size above which delivery costs nothing.
- **Minimum order**: the basket size below which a small order fee is added. Checkout still works.
- **Melt guarantee**: the sample promise that a melted order can be reported and replaced or refunded. The rule is ours, not Aice's.
- **Late guarantee**: the sample promise that the delivery fee is returned when an order arrives more than 10 minutes late. The rule is ours, not Aice's.
- **Demo controls**: the hidden panel used in the meeting: speed up tracking, make the order late, reset the demo.
- **Consumer**: the person who orders in the app.
- **Admin**: the person who runs the shop side and sees the orders.
